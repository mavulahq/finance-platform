# MAVULA Contributor License Agreement

MAVULA may require a Contributor License Agreement for non-trivial
contributions before merge.

Until automated CLA checks are configured, maintainers should request written
confirmation in the pull request that the contributor has the right to submit
the contribution and agrees to license it under the target package license.

No contribution may include confidential code, third-party proprietary material,
secrets, customer data or content that the contributor is not authorized to
license.
